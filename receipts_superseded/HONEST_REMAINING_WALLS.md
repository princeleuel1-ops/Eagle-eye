## W1 — No neural transformer was trained (IMPLEMENTED_NOT_EXECUTED at paper scale)
The sequential-prefix and Set-MT candidates are **algorithmic empirical-Bayes
analogs** of the paper's two architectures, sharing the exact interface
(`compiled prior context -> per-intervention predictive distributions`) a
neural predictor would implement. They reproduce the paper's structural
phenomena (order sensitivity, pooling pathology, prior-transfer value at low
target evidence, negative transfer under shift) and are measured against an
exact hierarchical Bayes oracle. What they are NOT: an amortized in-context
learner trained on 10M sequences. This sandbox has no GPU and no PyTorch;
training even a reduced transformer within the run budget would have consumed
the whole window for weaker evidence than the falsification platform provides.
Scale-up path: implement `predict_neural(compiled)` behind the same
`_package()` contract; the court, governor, evidence chain, and cockpit run
unchanged.
